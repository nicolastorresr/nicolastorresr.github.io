01 — ABOUT

Research with a
practical purpose.

I am a professor and researcher at the Department of Electronics of the Universidad Técnica Federico Santa María (UTFSM), Chile. My work focuses on designing and evaluating intelligent systems that combine machine learning, human-centered design and real-world applications.

My interests span AI, computer vision, generative models, LLMs, recommender systems and educational technology. I particularly enjoy turning research ideas into working prototypes, experiments and tools that can be used by people.
